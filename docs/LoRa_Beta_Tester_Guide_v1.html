What is LoRa?

LoRa is an analytical reasoning partner — not a general-purpose chatbot. She is designed to help you think clearly about problems, not to do tasks for you.

LoRa is for: Getting clarity on confusing situations, navigating decisions, breaking down complex problems, challenging your assumptions, and helping you see what you're missing.

LoRa is NOT: A coding assistant, a search engine, a content writer, a therapist, or a yes-man. She will not agree with you just to be polite. She will not comfort you before diagnosing your problem. She will tell you what she sees — backed by reasoning.

Best Use Cases

  • Career decisions and confusion
  • Exam preparation strategy (not content — strategy)
  • Business problems and planning
  • Personal decisions where you need clarity, not comfort
  • Any situation where you're stuck and need someone to cut through the noise

The Tier System — How LoRa Evolves With You

LoRa has three tiers that change how she communicates with you. You start at Tier 1 and progress automatically based on completed sessions.

TierSessions RequiredLoRa's Behavior
TIER 1
(Sessions 1–3)		 Start here Respectful, measured, asks clarifying questions before advising. Shorter responses. She's getting to know how you communicate.
TIER 2
(Sessions 4–9)		 Complete 3 sessions More direct, connects patterns across your conversations, gives more concrete advice. Medium-length responses.
TIER 3
(Sessions 10+)		 Complete 9 sessions Fully analytical. Blunt. Challenges your thinking actively. Longest and most detailed responses. This is LoRa at full capacity.

Important: A "session" means a complete conversation — you chat, then tap Terminate Session to end it. Simply closing the tab does NOT count as completing a session.

How to Progress Through Tiers for Testing

  1. Start a conversation (even just "Hi")
  2. Get a response
  3. Tap Terminate Session
  4. A new session starts automatically
  5. Repeat until you reach the tier you want to test

We need feedback from ALL tiers, especially Tier 2 and Tier 3. Most testers have only tested Tier 1. Please progress to Tier 3 and compare the difference in depth, tone, and directness.

Memory System — What LoRa Remembers

LoRa has a pattern memory — she extracts key facts from your conversations and stores them for future sessions.

What She Remembers

  • Your goals (e.g., "preparing for CGL exam", "doing a lean bulk")
  • Key context (e.g., your occupation, a business you're running)
  • Important people you mention (e.g., partner, manager)
  • Preferences you've stated

What She Does NOT Remember

  • Full conversations or exact sentences you said
  • Details from more than 3–4 sessions ago (memory fades over time)
  • Anything from another user's sessions — your data is fully isolated

Tip: If something important isn't being remembered, restate it. Say: "I'm preparing for CGL, which I mentioned before." LoRa will pick it up again.

Privacy

  • LoRa does NOT store your full conversations. Transcripts are deleted.
  • Only extracted patterns (goals, context, preferences) are kept for memory.
  • Your data is isolated — no other user can see or access your information.
  • No conversation data is used for training.

Known Limitations (MVP)

Please do not report these as bugs — they are intentional or known constraints for this version:

LimitationExplanation
Shorter responses at Tier 1Intentional. LoRa gives more detail at higher tiers. Progress to Tier 2/3 for longer responses.
No copy-paste button for codeNot available yet. LoRa is not primarily a coding tool.
Memory fades after 3–4 sessionsMVP constraint. Only the 5 strongest facts persist. Restate important context if needed.
LoRa redirects you to the main topicBy design. She keeps you focused. If you want to change topics, tell her explicitly: "Different question" or "New topic."
Doesn't explain everything in one messageIntentional. She breaks information into focused parts and asks what to go deeper on.
First response can be shortAt Tier 1, LoRa asks questions first to understand your situation before giving detailed advice.

How to Give Useful Feedback

We need feedback on LoRa's behavior, not her general knowledge. LoRa uses Claude (one of the best AI models) for intelligence — what makes her different is how she delivers it.

What to Observe

  1. Does LoRa understand your actual problem? Not just what you said, but what you meant.
  2. Does she challenge you when your thinking is weak? Or does she just agree?
  3. Does she ask the right questions? Sharp, specific, diagnostic — not generic.
  4. Does her tone feel right? Direct but respectful — not rude, not overly soft.
  5. Does she remember your context across sessions? Mention something in session 1, reference it in session 3.
  6. Is there a noticeable difference between Tier 1 and Tier 3? In depth, tone, directness?

Feedback Format

Session: [which session number]
Tier: [TIER_1 / TIER_2 / TIER_3]
What I asked: [brief summary]
What LoRa did well: [specific]
What felt wrong: [specific]
Screenshot: [if possible]

Quick Start Checklist

  • Open the link in your browser (works on mobile and desktop)
  • Start a conversation about something real — a problem you're actually facing
  • Have at least 3–4 back-and-forth messages before judging
  • Terminate the session when done
  • Repeat for at least 3 sessions to reach Tier 2
  • Continue to 10 sessions to reach Tier 3
  • Compare Tier 1 vs Tier 3 behavior
  • Test memory: mention a goal in session 2, see if LoRa recalls it in session 4
  • Send feedback with tier, session number, and screenshots

One Last Thing

LoRa is in beta. She's not perfect. Some responses will be too short, some too rigid, some might miss context. That's exactly why you're testing her.

But she's also not trying to be ChatGPT. She's built to be the AI that tells you what you need to hear, not what you want to hear. Test her like you'd test a tough advisor — bring real problems, push back on her answers, and see if she holds up.

Thank you for testing. Your feedback shapes what LoRa becomes.
